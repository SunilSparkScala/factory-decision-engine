# Incident Report: Minor Dimensional Variance Following Rapid Transfer

## 1. Incident Overview
During an emergency transfer of stamping operations from M01 to M02 at Station A1, minor panel flange spring-back variance occurred on the first batch due to skipped die pre-heating.

## 2. Corrective Action
- Die pre-heating procedure (KB-MNT-004) was mandated as an unbypassable step for all subsequent machine transfers.
