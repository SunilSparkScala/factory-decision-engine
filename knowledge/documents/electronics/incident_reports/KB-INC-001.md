# Incident Report: M17 Unplanned Downtime & Recovery Execution

## 1. Incident Overview
On Q3 2026 Shift 2, Machine M17 (Pick-and-place Unit 2 on Line B) stopped after a placement head vacuum fault. Downtime duration was 8.0 hours.

## 2. Recovery Action Taken
- Production workload (NW-Speaker main board placement) was temporarily transferred to alternative machine M18 on Line B (Station B2) and M05 on Line A.
- First Article Inspection (FAI) was completed cleanly within 35 minutes.
- All high-priority orders (O1038, O1044) met delivery deadlines without customer disruption.

## 3. Lessons Learned
- Keeping feeder setups pre-kitted for alternative machines at Station B2 (M16, M18) significantly reduced capacity loss.
