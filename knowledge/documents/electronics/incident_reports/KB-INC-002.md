# Incident Report: Solder Bridging Following Rapid Transfer

## 1. Incident Overview
During an emergency transfer of solder paste printing from M01 to M02 at Station A1, the first panels showed solder bridging because the stencil was not cleaned and the paste was not conditioned to room temperature.

## 2. Corrective Action
- The changeover procedure (KB-MNT-004) now makes stencil cleaning and paste conditioning an unbypassable step for all printer transfers.
