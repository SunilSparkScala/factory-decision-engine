# Plant Preventive Maintenance Schedule & Guidelines

## 1. Preventive Maintenance Intervals
- **Weekly PM (2 hours):** Nozzle and camera cleaning, conveyor belt check, filter inspection.
- **Monthly PM (6 hours):** Placement head calibration, reflow oven profile verification, cabinet thermal inspection.
- **Quarterly PM (12 hours):** Major overhaul, gantry lubrication, ESD grounding audit.

## 2. Scheduling Constraints
- PM windows should be scheduled during planned non-production shifts or weekend slots where possible.
- If a machine needs emergency maintenance during a PM window, emergency repairs take precedence.
