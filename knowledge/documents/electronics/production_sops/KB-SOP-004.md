# Production SOP: High-Priority Delivery Order Expediting

## 1. Definition
High-priority orders are committed retail and distributor contracts with strict delivery penalties.

## 2. Mitigation Rules
- If a machine failure affects 1 or more HIGH priority orders, overall delivery risk is escalated to `HIGH`.
- Recovery plans must prioritize capacity recovery for HIGH priority orders before LOW/MEDIUM priority orders.
