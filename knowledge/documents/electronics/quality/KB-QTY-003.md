# Quality Precautions When Running Degraded Equipment

## 1. Overview
Operating equipment with a reduced health score (60.0–85.0%) introduces process variance.

## 2. Precautions
- **Sampling Frequency:** Increase automated optical inspection to 100% of finished boards.
- **Process Constraints:** Do not run fine-pitch components (0201 / fine-pitch BGA) on degraded placement machinery.
