# Quality Assurance Guidelines: Post-Transfer First Article Inspection

## 1. Requirement
Whenever production is transferred to an alternative machine (e.g. from M17 to M05 or M16), a mandatory First Article Inspection (FAI) must be performed.

## 2. Protocol
- **Sample Size:** First 3 consecutive boards produced on the target machine.
- **Measurements:** Automated optical inspection (AOI) of placement and solder joints; X-ray check for hidden joints.
- **Approval:** A Quality Engineer must sign off on FAI before bulk production release.
- **Proxy Health Penalty:** If the target machine health score is below 80%, FAI sample size increases to 10 boards.
