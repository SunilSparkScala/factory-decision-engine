# Quality Hold & Containment Standard Operating Procedure

## 1. Trigger Conditions
A Quality Hold must be initiated immediately if:
- 2 consecutive solder or placement defects are detected at inspection stations (e.g. Station C4).
- A machine failure occurs mid-cycle, leaving a board partially processed.

## 2. Containment Protocol
1. Quarantine all boards produced on the affected machine since the last verified clean inspection.
2. Apply RED HOLD tags and move boards to the ESD-safe quarantine area.
3. Conduct 100% inspection or rework.
