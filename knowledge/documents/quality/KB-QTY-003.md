# Quality Precautions When Running Degraded Equipment

## 1. Overview
Operating equipment with reduced health score (60.0–85.0%) introduces potential process variance.

## 2. Precautions
- **Sampling Frequency:** Increase automated vision check sampling to 100% of finished assemblies.
- **Process Constraints:** Do not attempt high-precision tight-tolerance operations on degraded stamping or welding machinery.
