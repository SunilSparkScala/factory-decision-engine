# Quality Hold & Containment Standard Operating Procedure

## 1. Trigger Conditions
A Quality Hold must be initiated immediately if:
- 2 consecutive dimensional non-conformances are detected at inspection stations (e.g. Station C4).
- A machine failure occurs mid-cycle, leaving a workpiece partially processed.

## 2. Containment Protocol
1. Quarantine all parts produced on the affected machine since the last verified clean inspection.
2. Apply physical RED HOLD tags and segregate items in designated quarantine area.
3. Conduct 100% sorting or rework.
