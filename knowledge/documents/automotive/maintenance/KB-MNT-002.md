# Operating Guidelines for Degraded Machinery (Health Score 60–85%)

## 1. Overview
When a machine enters `DEGRADED` status (health score between 60.0% and 85.0%), immediate replacement is not required, but operating restrictions must be applied to prevent total breakdown.

## 2. Mandatory Restrictions
- **Speed Derating:** Reduce line speed / cycle rate by 15–20%.
- **Thermal Monitoring:** Continuous infrared temperature logging on motor drives.
- **Inspection Frequency:** Increase quality sampling frequency from 1-in-50 to 1-in-10 units.
- **Max Shift Hours:** Degraded machinery must not exceed 12 consecutive hours without a 1-hour cooling window.
