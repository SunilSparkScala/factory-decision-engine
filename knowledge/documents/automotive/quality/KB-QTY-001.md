# Quality Assurance Guidelines: Post-Transfer First Article Inspection

## 1. Requirement
Whenever production operations are transferred to an alternative machine (e.g. from M17 to M05 or M16), a mandatory First Article Inspection (FAI) must be performed.

## 2. Protocol
- **Sample Size:** First 3 consecutive parts produced on the target machine.
- **Measurements:** Dimensional check using CMM or 3D optical scanner; weld seam integrity via ultrasonic test.
- **Approval:** Quality Quality Engineer must sign off on FAI before bulk production release.
- **Proxy Health Penalty:** If target machine health score is below 80%, FAI sample size increases to 10 parts.
