# Plant Preventive Maintenance Schedule & Guidelines

## 1. Preventive Maintenance Intervals
- **Weekly PM (2 hours):** Sensor cleaning, belt tension check, fluid level top-off.
- **Monthly PM (6 hours):** Robot recalibration, hydraulic filter replacement, electrical cabinet thermal inspection.
- **Quarterly PM (12 hours):** Major overhaul, gear oil replacement, structural bolt torque audit.

## 2. Scheduling Constraints
- PM windows must be scheduled during planned non-production shifts or weekend maintenance slots where possible.
- If a machine enters emergency maintenance during PM window, emergency repairs take precedence.
