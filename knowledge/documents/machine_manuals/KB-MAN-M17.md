# M17 Heavy Duty Welding Cell — Operating Characteristics & Constraints

## 1. Description & Location
M17 is a heavy-duty multi-robot welding cell stationed at Station B2 (Line B, SUV Line). It is configured for heavy frame joining for Model-E and Model-C SUV chassis assemblies.

## 2. Key Performance Metrics
- **Nominal Capacity:** 38.6–42.5 units/hour
- **Cycle Time:** 85.0–93.0 seconds
- **Health Rating:** 95.0% (Nominal Baseline)
- **Primary Consumable:** MAT_03 Welding Wire & CO2/Argon shielding gas mix

## 3. Machine Downtime & Recovery Guidelines
- **Unplanned Failure Mode:** Servo-motor overheating on Axis 3 is the most common cause of downtime on M17.
- **Cool-down Requirement:** 2.0 hours minimum cooling required prior to servo replacement.
- **Transfer Alternatives:** Work assigned to M17 can be transferred to M16, M18 on Line B, or M04, M05, M06 on Line A (Station A2), or M25, M26 on Line C (Station C1).

## 4. Quality Precautions
- Following any stoppage exceeding 4 hours, 5 sample welds must undergo ultrasonic non-destructive testing before mass production resume.
