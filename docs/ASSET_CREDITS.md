# Asset credits

The app ships no photos, videos or third-party images. Everything on screen is drawn in HTML/CSS
or inline SVG, and all plant data is synthetic (generated by `scripts/generate_data.py`).

| Asset | Source | Licence |
|---|---|---|
| Logo and icons | Inline SVG drawn for this project, plus standard Unicode symbols | Project's own |
| Plus Jakarta Sans | Google Fonts | SIL Open Font License 1.1 |
| Inter | Google Fonts | SIL Open Font License 1.1 |
| JetBrains Mono | Google Fonts | SIL Open Font License 1.1 |

Earlier builds used stock video on the landing page; it was removed because the clips were not
representative of the product (one showed a car body line, the other a branded robot without a
recorded licence).
