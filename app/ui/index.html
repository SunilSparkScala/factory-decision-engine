            How Factory Decision Engine Works

            01

            AI Reasons, Python Calculates

            Every operational number (capacity loss, schedule delay, energy consumption, Pareto score) is computed by a deterministic optimization engine in Python. Gemini never invents numerical values.

            02

            Grounded Specialist War Room

            Four autonomous specialist agents (Production, Quality, Maintenance, Energy) evaluate the candidate recovery actions against 18 engineering documents and standards before formulating the decision brief.

            03

            Mandatory Human Approval

            The system is strictly an intelligence tool, not an autonomous controller. Human shift managers review trade-offs, verify citations, and explicitly approve the action before dispatch notices are created.
